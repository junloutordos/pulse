# Pilot: matched planning tasks (stretch target)

Three PSHS-CRC staff each do the same two tasks twice: once with the current manual process, once with PULSE. Use the same approved aggregates for both. Record times in `timing-log.md`.

**Task A.** Using the 2026 cycle data, name the five municipalities where you would focus NCE outreach, and say in one line why for each.

**Task B.** For the municipality you ranked first, prepare a one-page note a planning meeting could use: what the numbers are, how complete the data is, what you would do next, and who is responsible.

**Scoring**
- Preparation time, from receiving the data to a finished answer.
- Accuracy: every number in the answer reconciles with the approved source.
- Usability: three questions, 1 (poor) to 5 (good): "I could explain my choice to a colleague", "I trusted the numbers", "I would use this next cycle".
- At least one actual outreach-planning decision supported by the tool, recorded in the action register.

Target from the proposal: at least 50% lower median preparation time with full reconciliation to the approved totals. These are evaluation targets, not assumed outcomes.
